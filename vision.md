# Project Vision

## Personal AI Investment Analyst

### Vision

Build a privacy-first AI investment assistant that helps users perform professional-grade fundamental analysis by combining financial data, regulatory filings, market news, and personal investment knowledge.

Rather than acting as an automated stock picker, the system should function like a team of experienced financial analysts that can research, evaluate, and explain investment opportunities while keeping the user fully in control of all decisions.

The platform should be modular, extensible, and provider-independent, supporting both local and cloud-based language models.

---

# Objectives

The assistant should be able to:

* Analyze publicly traded companies.
* Read and summarize annual and quarterly reports.
* Explain complex financial statements.
* Calculate and interpret financial ratios.
* Evaluate valuation metrics.
* Analyze macroeconomic developments.
* Monitor market news.
* Identify opportunities and risks.
* Support long-term investment decisions.
* Learn from the user's own research and investment philosophy.

---

# Core Capabilities

## Company Analysis

Generate comprehensive reports including:

* Business model overview
* Competitive landscape
* SWOT analysis
* Porter's Five Forces
* Economic moat assessment
* Management evaluation
* Dividend history
* Insider transactions
* Growth opportunities
* Key business risks

---

## Financial Analysis

Automatically calculate and explain:

* Revenue growth
* Earnings growth
* Free Cash Flow
* Operating margin
* Gross margin
* Net margin
* Return on Equity (ROE)
* Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)
* Debt ratios
* Liquidity ratios
* Piotroski F-Score
* Altman Z-Score
* Beneish M-Score
* Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)
* Relative valuation multiples

---

## Regulatory Filings

Analyze documents such as:

* Annual reports
* Quarterly reports
* SEC 10-K
* SEC 10-Q
* Earnings releases
* Investor presentations

The assistant should identify:

* Material changes
* New risks
* Strategy changes
* Capital allocation decisions
* Management commentary
* Accounting changes

---

## News Analysis

Continuously monitor financial news and classify:

* Positive developments
* Negative developments
* Market sentiment
* Earnings expectations
* Industry trends
* Macroeconomic impacts

Every news item should be linked to the relevant companies and investment thesis.

---

## Portfolio Intelligence

Provide insights including:

* Portfolio overview
* Sector allocation
* Geographic diversification
* Dividend projections
* Risk concentration
* Watchlists
* Rebalancing suggestions
* Position summaries

---

# Multi-Agent Architecture

Instead of relying on a single AI model, the platform should support specialized agents.

Examples include:

* Fundamental Analyst
* Financial Statement Analyst
* Valuation Analyst
* Macro Economist
* News Analyst
* Risk Manager
* Portfolio Advisor

A coordinating "Chief Investment Officer" agent should combine the findings into a single, coherent investment report.

---

# Data Sources

The platform should support multiple data providers.

Examples include:

* SEC EDGAR
* Financial Modeling Prep
* Alpha Vantage
* Yahoo Finance
* Stooq
* OpenFIGI
* OpenInsider
* FRED
* Commercial news providers (optional)
* User-owned documents and research notes

No single provider should be required.

---

# Design Principles

* Privacy first
* Local-first architecture whenever possible
* Provider independence
* Modular design
* Explainable AI
* Transparent calculations
* Reproducible analysis
* Human decision support
* No investment advice

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to build a personal AI research platform that dramatically reduces the time required to analyze companies while improving consistency, transparency, and depth of research.

Instead of replacing the investor, the system should augment human decision-making by automating repetitive analysis, surfacing relevant information, and presenting well-structured investment theses supported by verifiable data.

Ultimately, the platform should evolve into a comprehensive AI-powered investment research environment capable of integrating structured financial data, unstructured documents, market news, portfolio analytics, and autonomous research agents into a single, extensible ecosystem.
